## Authored for Manabi

The 155 grammar points, 13 reading passages, kana mnemonics and confusable
sets, and the verified pitch-accent entries were written for this project and
are covered by its licence.

Verb conjugations are generated from the dictionary form and verb group by the
godan/ichidan rules; the generator was validated against hand-written
reference entries before being applied to the full set.
